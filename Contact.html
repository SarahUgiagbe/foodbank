About We Share & Care Food Bank

We Share & Care Food Bank is a community-driven organisation dedicated to fighting food insecurity in our region. Founded on the belief that no one should go hungry, we rely on the generosity of our volunteers and donors to distribute meals and groceries to families in need — five days a week, every week of the year.
